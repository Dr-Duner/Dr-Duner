# LinkedIn Content

LinkedIn is the formal channel where dental owners/office managers
actually are. Goal: content that produces inbound DMs, not vanity likes.
General channel strategy lives in `social-media-marketing`; this is the
LinkedIn craft layer.

## Profile = landing page (set once)
- Headline: outcome + niche, not title. e.g. "I help dental practices
  reply to every Google review — HIPAA-safe, in their voice."
- Featured: the 30-sec demo clip + compliance one-pager.
- About: the wedge (generic AI confirms patients = HIPAA violation), a
  clear "DM me 'reviews'" CTA.

## Post formats that work here (ranked)
1. **Text + 1 image/clip:** a real before/after — generic robotic reply
   vs. our voice-matched, HIPAA-safe reply. Highest converting.
2. **Document/carousel:** "3 things a dental office legally can't say
   in a Google review reply" → teach, soft CTA last slide.
3. **Build-in-public text:** a real number or lesson from running the
   service. Credibility in a compliance niche.
4. **Short story post:** a (anonymized, no PHI) cautionary tale of a
   bad auto-reply, then the safe way.

## Writing rules
- Hook in line 1, before the "...see more" fold. No preamble.
- One idea per post. Short lines, whitespace, no walls of text.
- Plain professional voice; no hashtag spam (2–3 max), no engagement
  bait ("comment YES"), no fake metrics.
- End with a soft, specific CTA: "If you run a dental practice and
  reviews pile up, DM me 'reviews' — I'll handle your last 10 free,
  HIPAA-safe, you do nothing."
- Never reference any patient/clinical info, only public review facts.

## Comment & DM motion
- Spend 15 min/day commenting usefully on dental practice-management
  posts — visibility without pitching.
- When someone engages, move to DM with the free-batch offer → hand to
  `sales-closing`. Don't pitch in public comments.

## Cadence
2–3 posts/week (1 before/after, 1 teach, 1 build-in-public). Repurpose
each idea from `social-media-marketing` into LinkedIn's format. Track
which post types produce DMs; do more of those.
