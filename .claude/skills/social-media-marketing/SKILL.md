# Social Media Marketing

How this business gets seen and gets its first customers.

## Channels, ranked for this niche
1. **X (build-in-public):** the source idea came from X; that audience
   buys productized services. Post the build, the HIPAA insight, demos.
2. **LinkedIn:** practice owners + dental office-manager groups; more
   formal, ROI/risk framing.
3. **Local + niche communities:** dental office-manager Facebook
   groups, r/Dentistry / r/dentaloffice (give value, don't spam),
   dental practice-management forums.
4. **Direct demo clips:** 30-sec recording: a nasty Google review →
   safe, warm reply → one-tap approve on a phone → posted. "We write &
   post; they just tap yes." Sell the managed outcome, not a tool.
   Single most converting asset.

## Post patterns that work here
- **The dangerous-default hook:** "Most AI review responders just
  committed a HIPAA violation for this dental office. Here's the reply
  they generated 👇" → show bad vs. our safe version.
- **Build-in-public:** ship updates, real numbers, lessons. Honest > hype.
- **Before/after:** robotic generic reply vs. in-their-voice safe reply.
- **Teach, don't pitch:** "3 things a dental office can't say in a
  Google reply" → soft CTA.

## Rules
- One idea per post. Hook in the first line. Show, don't claim.
- Always a concrete artifact (screenshot/clip) > text claims.
- No fake metrics, no engagement bait. Credibility is the asset in a
  compliance-sensitive niche.
- Soft CTA only ("DM 'reviews' if you run a dental office"). The
  conversion happens in DMs — hand off to `cold-outreach-dental`.

## Cadence
3–5 build-in-public posts/week on X, 1–2 LinkedIn, 1 demo clip/week,
value posts (not pitches) in communities. Repurpose one insight across
all channels.
