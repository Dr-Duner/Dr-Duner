  grounded, never shrill, never bubbly.

- Pace slow, generous pauses. Breath audible, intimate (close-mic).
- Reference feel: a wise older friend at dawn, not a narrator.

## Per-shot script (VO + on-screen mono)

### SHOT 1 — COLD OPEN: NOVA'S MORNING · 0–4s
- VISUAL: wide, chibi Nova at the bronze telescope, marking stars on a
  wax tablet, unaware. 8% slow push-in, idle breathing, light motes.
- AUDIO: soft sea + dawn birds. No music yet.
- VO (Nova, quiet, almost to herself): "Every morning I measure the
  sky. And every morning the sky ignores me."

### SHOT 2 — THE BUZZ · 4–7s
- VISUAL: cut tighter — modern phone on the marble ledge, dark screen →
  single green pulse. Nova's head snaps, surprise pop. Light-streak wipe.
- AUDIO: ambient ducks; one clean modern buzz (the hook).
- VO: (none — let the buzz land.)

### SHOT 3 — THE READ (mono) · 7–11s
- VISUAL: phone fills frame, Nova's chibi face at the edge. IBM Plex
  Mono types on, green on navy.
- ON-SCREEN: `> THETA: you think you control the outcome.`
  `> you don't.`
- VO (Nova, reading it back low and even): "You think you control the
  outcome. You don't."

### SHOT 4 — TEACHING A: HOOK + STOIC · 11–20s
- VISUAL: constellations dissolve into a live green candlestick chart.
  Split label: PROCESS | OUTCOME. Green line-draw builds the split.
- ON-SCREEN (mono): `Some things are up to us; some are not. —Epictetus`
- VO (Nova, calm): "The chart is not yours. Your method is. Two
  thousand years ago a slave wrote that down. It still holds."

### SHOT 5 — TEACHING B: CRAFT + INTERACTIVE · 20–31s
- VISUAL: user sorts four cards into CONTROL vs NOISE. CONTROL =
  research / size / entry / the exit (4 marble stones). NOISE = price /
  earnings / the storm at sea. On sort, proof columns ease in:
  +98% lucky-bad-process vs −8% sound-process.
- ON-SCREEN (mono): `CONTROL: research · size · entry · exit`
  `NOISE: price · news · the storm`
- VO (Nova, as the user sorts): "A win can be bad investing. A loss can
  be good. Sort what's yours from what never was."

### SHOT 6 — SCAN: LIVE CARD (1:1 app UI) · 31–36s
- VISUAL: a real scanner card. Editable = size / entry / exit (signal
  green). Read-only + dimmed = price / news ("not yours"). Defined risk
  pre-filled. Nova: small approving glance to camera.
- ON-SCREEN: the shipping scanner card UI (DESIGN §3b/§3c — must match).
- VO (Nova, low, sure): "This is the only part you touch. Make it
  deserve the trade."

### SHOT 7 — COACH LAND + Θ CTA · 36–40s
- VISUAL: Nova sets the phone down, returns to the telescope; camera
  pulls back — ancient + modern in one frame. Θ draws on in green
  candlesticks.
- ON-SCREEN: `OPEN THETA — trading, made usable today.`
- VO (Nova, the one line — softest, lands it): "You don't control the
  price. You control whether you deserved the trade."
