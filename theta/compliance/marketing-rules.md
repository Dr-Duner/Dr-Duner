# Theta — Marketing Copy Rules

**Status: working compliance control — 2026-05-20.** Closes
`COMPLIANCE.md RF-05` (AI-washing) and the marketing portion of
`RF-08`. Must be applied to every public-facing piece of copy — App
Store listings, web pages, social posts, podcast appearances, paid
ads, email — before publication.

---

## Why this exists
Two enforcement regimes drive these rules:
1. **SEC anti-fraud (§206(4) and §17(a))** — false or misleading
   statements about an investment product are actionable whether or
   not Theta is a registered adviser. Recent precedent: SEC
   Enforcement charges against Delphia and Global Predictions
   (March 2024, ~$400k combined) for "AI washing."
2. **FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) + ROSCA + general
   prohibition on deceptive acts** — applies to all consumer
   marketing.

Stoic frame: *truth in advertising is itself a virtue.* This isn't
a friction; it's mission-aligned.

---

## Forbidden language
Do **not** ever use, in any public-facing copy:

### About AI
- "AI-powered investment advice."
- "Our AI predicts the market."
- "AI-driven trading signals."
- "AI that learns your portfolio."
- "Beat the market with AI."
- "Our proprietary AI."
- Any phrase that implies the AI makes investment decisions or
  predictions.

### About returns / performance
- "Make $X/month with Theta."
- "Users have averaged Y% returns."
- "Doubled my account."
- "Risk-free returns."
- "Guaranteed profits / wins / income."
- Any percentage or dollar amount associated with user outcomes.
- "Win rate" of any strategy unless presented with full
  hypothetical-performance disclaimer (see
  `legal/persistent-disclaimers.md D-6`) AND reviewed by counsel.
- Cherry-picked screenshots of winning paper trades without the
  matching losers.

### About what Theta is
- "Investment adviser." "Advisor" / "personal advisor."
- "Broker."
- "Trading platform" (Theta does not execute; misleading).
- "Trading signals" (implies pick generation).
- "Hot stocks." "Stock picks." "Trade alerts." "Buy/sell signals."
- "Insider knowledge." "Edge over the market."

### About users
- Specific testimonials with named individuals' returns ("Jane made
  $5,000 in 2 weeks").
- "Join thousands of profitable traders."
- "Our community averages …"
- Implying that following Theta's coaching produces wealth.

## Approved language
Use this vocabulary instead:

### About the technology
- "A chat-based coach powered by language-model technology."
- "Language-model coaches that teach options strategy."
- "The coach explains strategies and reflects your own rules back
  to you."
- Lead with the **character** (Lyra, Nestor, Chiron, Atlas), not
  the technology. The product is "Lyra teaches you options," not
  "AI teaches you options."

### About what Theta is
- "A strategy education and analytics SaaS tool."
- "An options-trading discipline app."
- "A Stoic-framed strategy builder, scanner, journal, and coach."
- "We teach strategies; you make the decisions."
- "Education and analytics — not investment advice; not a broker."

### About what users can do
- "Build your own options trading strategy."
- "Test it against the market with paper trades."
- "See how your strategy holds up to your real trades."
- "Learn to think about options the way disciplined traders do."

### About results
- "Helps you understand options strategies."
- "Build the discipline disciplined options traders use."
- "Track adherence to the rules you set for yourself."

## Mandatory disclosure obligations
- **AI disclosure (honest, not buried):** somewhere prominent on
  the marketing surface (footer of homepage, "How it works" page),
  state: *"Theta's coaches are language-model interfaces trained
  to teach options strategy. They do not provide personalized
  investment advice."* Do not hide it; do not lead with it.
- **Risk disclosure link:** every public-facing surface that
  discusses trading must link to (or include) the disclaimer in
  `legal/persistent-disclaimers.md D-10`.
- **No-advice footer:** every marketing email, social post (where
  character count permits), and ad creative must include some
  variant of "Educational. Not investment advice. Options trading
  involves substantial risk of loss."

## Endorsement / testimonial rules (FTC 16 CFR 255)
**Day-one rule under bootstrap: no paid testimonials, no influencer
endorsements, no affiliate-paid creators.** This eliminates 90% of
the compliance burden.

If/when we add paid endorsements later:
- Every endorser must be a real user with real experience.
- Material connection (payment, free service) must be clearly
  disclosed in the same medium as the endorsement. "#ad" alone is
  insufficient; disclosure must be clear and conspicuous within the
  post.
- Endorser's experience must be typical, OR the typical experience
  must be disclosed alongside.
- No fake reviews. No reviews on Theta's own website unless they
  pass the same disclosure rules.

---

## Performance / track-record rules
- Do not advertise Theta's own track record.
- Do not advertise any user's track record.
- Do not advertise a strategy's win rate without (a) full
  hypothetical-performance disclaimer, (b) the time period and
  conditions, (c) counsel review.
- Paper-trade leaderboards are forbidden for cash prizes;
  adherence-score leaderboards (rewarding discipline, not P&L) are
  acceptable. Display of adherence leaderboards in marketing is
  acceptable; display of P&L leaderboards is not.

---

## Comparative claims
If we compare Theta to other products (brokers, courses, scanners):
- Compare on factual, verifiable features ("Theta scans on the
  user's own strategy; thinkorswim Scan and Finviz also do this.").
- Do not claim superior outcomes.
- Do not denigrate competitors with unsubstantiated claims.

## Example transformations
| Bad | Good |
|-----|------|
| "AI coach picks your trades" | "Lyra teaches you the strategy; you pick your trades" |
| "AI-powered trade signals" | "A scanner that runs the strategy you built" |
| "Make consistent income with Theta" | "Build the discipline disciplined options traders use" |
| "Our AI beats the market" | "Theta is an educational SaaS tool. We don't predict the market." |
| "73% win rate" | (Remove. Or only with hypothetical-performance disclaimer + counsel sign-off.) |
| "Jane made $5,000 with Theta" | (Remove. No testimonial returns claims.) |
| "Get insider picks" | (Remove. No "picks" language.) |
| "AI advisor in your pocket" | "An options-strategy coach in your pocket — not an advisor" |

## Pre-publication review checklist
Before publishing any public-facing copy, confirm:
- [ ] No forbidden phrases (above list).
- [ ] No specific dollar amounts or percentages associated with
      user outcomes.
- [ ] No "AI" used as a noun-of-action ("AI does X for you").
- [ ] Lead with character / education, not technology.
- [ ] Risk disclaimer present where required.
- [ ] No paid testimonials without FTC-compliant disclosure (zero
      such uses on day one).
- [ ] No comparative outcome claims.
- [ ] Specific-ticker mentions limited to public-domain general
      examples (e.g., "an iron condor on a stock like SPY for
      illustration"); never as a trade idea or recommendation.
- [ ] Counsel review triggered if: (a) any performance / track-
      record claim, (b) any new "what Theta does" framing not
      previously approved, (c) any influencer / affiliate
      arrangement, (d) any new country.
