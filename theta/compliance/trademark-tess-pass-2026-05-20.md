## Headline finding
**The bare "Theta" name has meaningful trademark risk** in the
options-trading education space for two converging reasons:

1. **Descriptiveness (Trademark Act §2(e)(1)).** "Theta" is the
   standard industry name for the Greek letter representing time
   decay of an option. USPTO is likely to reject a bare THETA mark
   for options-related goods/services as merely descriptive of a
   feature of the product, unless we can show acquired
   distinctiveness ("secondary meaning") under §2(f) — which
   generally requires 5+ years of substantially exclusive use.
2. **Existing use by direct and adjacent competitors** in the
   options-trading education space, particularly **Theta Trading
   Co.** (Stock Options Academy, ~1,300+ students, founded 2019)
   and **ThetaData** (options-market data provider). Both have at
   minimum common-law trademark rights in their geographic/product
   markets from use in commerce, regardless of whether USPTO
   registration was sought.

This is not a deal-breaker, but it changes the trademark
strategy: **don't rely on bare "Theta" alone.** See
recommendations below.

## Descriptiveness analysis
Trademark Act §2(e)(1) bars registration of marks that are
"merely descriptive" of the goods/services. The USPTO and TTAB
look at whether the mark "immediately conveys" a characteristic of
the product to an ordinary purchaser.

For an options-trading product, "Theta" immediately conveys "the
Greek measuring time decay of options" to anyone in the target
market. This is the *exact* meaning we're invoking with the brand
("Theta is the daily tax"). The descriptive character is the
brand premise.

USPTO would likely reject the bare THETA mark on §2(e)(1) grounds
for goods/services that include options trading, options data, or
options education. We would need to either:
- Demonstrate **acquired distinctiveness** under §2(f) (typically
  5+ years of substantially exclusive use + evidence of consumer
  association). We do not have this.
- File on the **Supplemental Register** (an inferior registration
  that doesn't give the same rights and doesn't bar third-party
  use in commerce — useful as a stepping stone but not a real
  trademark moat).
- Use a **compound mark** with additional distinctive matter
  (e.g., THETA COACH, THETA DISCIPLINE, THETA STOIC) that takes
  the whole mark out of merely-descriptive territory. Each
  compound mark needs to clear the existing use by Theta Trading
  Co. and others — see recommendations below.

## Recommendations (in order of preference)

### Option A — Distinctive compound mark with the Theta brand kept
Examples to evaluate with counsel:
- **THETA COACH** — likely runs into Theta Trading Co.'s "Theta
  Trading" mark (similar in commercial impression). Probably
  blocked.
- **THETA STOIC** — uncommon combination; distinctive secondary
  element ("Stoic" not used by competitors found in this pass).
  Plausible.
- **THETA DISCIPLINE** — same logic as STOIC; plausible.
- **THETA STRATEGY** — descriptive of services; weak.
- **THE THETA APP** — generic combination, weak.
- **THETA + a coined word** (e.g., "Thetalith," "Thetagora")
  — strongest distinctiveness, weakest brand recognition.

Counsel should run a full search on the chosen compound before
filing.

### Option B — Stylized Θ + design mark
The Greek letter Θ rendered in a distinctive way (the locked
black/signal-green design language is a candidate) can be filed as
a **design mark** rather than a word mark. This gives narrower
protection (only the specific design) but avoids the §2(e)(1)
problem entirely.

Practical use: file the design mark (the Θ logo) as the primary
trademark, and use "Theta" as the brand name in marketing without
asserting word-mark protection over the bare word.

### Option C — Rebrand
If counsel clearance on a compound mark fails, the cleanest
long-term path is a different primary name with "Theta" as a
descriptive sub-theme. Examples (not exhaustive — for counsel
brainstorming): a coined or arbitrary mark for the app itself,
with "Theta" used inside the product to refer to the symbol on
the strategy. This is the safest path but trades the brand
recognition we've been building in this repo.

### Option D — Coexistence with Theta Trading Co.
A coexistence agreement with Theta Trading Co. (and possibly
ThetaData) could allow both to operate in the field with
geographic, channel, or sub-category carve-outs. This requires
counsel-led negotiation; the existing players may decline.
