# Decision — Rebrand the primary name (2026-05-20, Gee)

Outcome of the trademark investigation in
`theta/compliance/trademark-tess-pass-2026-05-20.md`. Gee chose
Option C from the TESS pass: *"Rebrand the primary name with
Theta preserved as an internal sub-theme. Cleanest legally;
trades brand recognition."*

---

## The decision
The primary **product / app / company name will change.** The bare
word "Theta" will not be the outward brand. "Theta" is preserved
internally as:
1. The Greek letter measuring option time decay (its actual
   meaning — that's the option Greek the curriculum teaches).
2. The subject of L5 ("Theta — Time Is the Tax").
3. The stylized Θ as a potential design-mark element (the
   black/signal-green logo treatment locked in `DESIGN.md §2`).

---

## Why now (pre-launch is the cheapest time)
Two converging problems with the bare "Theta" name as a product
trademark:
1. **Descriptiveness (Trademark Act §2(e)(1)).** Theta = the
   options Greek for time decay. USPTO is likely to reject a
   bare THETA word mark for options-related goods/services as
   merely descriptive of a feature of the product.
2. **Existing direct competitor — Theta Trading Co.** (Burlington
   ON, founded 2019, "Stock Options Academy," ~1,300+ students).
   Common-law trademark rights in our direct field.

Rebranding pre-launch, pre-marketing, pre-domain, pre-app-store-
listing is dramatically cheaper than rebranding after any of those
are in market.

## Conceptual frame for the new name
The four coaches are teachers. The product is the *place / school
/ discipline* at which they teach. This gives the cleanest naming
frame: **the product is named for the school; the coaches are the
teachers within it.**

Examples of how this frames a name:
- "Stoa teaches you options." (the coaches teach at the Stoa.)
- "Welcome to Praxis." (the coaches train you in praxis.)
- "Open the Agora." (the coaches meet you at the Agora.)

This keeps Lyra, Nestor, Chiron, Atlas as the emotional spine while
giving the wrapper a distinctive, non-descriptive mark that USPTO
will accept and that doesn't collide with the existing Theta
Trading Co. presence.
