# Working with Claude Code from my phone

A quick reference for chatting back and forth during the day.

## The basics

- **It's async, like texting a coworker.** Send a message, lock the
  phone, come back later. A notification arrives when the task is done
  or a decision is needed.
- **The cloud container is temporary.** Work only survives if it's
  committed and pushed. Claude auto-saves at logical stopping points.
- **One clear goal per message.** Plain English is fine. Specific beats
  long and vague.

## A good daily rhythm

1. Describe what to build or change in one message.
2. Put the phone away — Claude works in the background.
3. Get a notification, review the plain-language summary.
4. Reply with tweaks, or "commit and push" to lock it in.

## Settings chosen

- Saving: **auto-commit and push at stopping points** (least friction
  on a phone).

## Handy phrases

- "Commit and push everything" — save before stopping.
- "Watch this PR" — Claude responds to review feedback / CI on its own.
- "Start fresh on <task>" — begin a new piece of work.
